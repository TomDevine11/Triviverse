Contact

Triviverse is run by one person, so replies are not instant — but every message is read.

General

hello@triviverse.com — questions, feedback, suggestions for new games, or anything else.

A question or answer looks wrong

Send it to hello@triviverse.com. The most useful thing you can include is which game it was, the date you played it, and what you expected the answer to be — that is usually enough to find the underlying data and fix it.

Privacy and data

privacy@triviverse.com for anything covered by the Privacy Policy, including questions about advertising or your consent choice.

If you want your leaderboard nickname, league memberships or scores removed, email the same address from any account and include your nickname and, if you can, the name of a league you are in. Scores are tied to an anonymous device id rather than an account, so that is how the right records are found.

Articles and figures

The articles are built from the same data as the games and each one says when its figures were checked. If a number has gone out of date or a record has been broken since, tell us at hello@triviverse.com and it will be corrected, with the article's "Updated" date changed to match.

Leagues, schools and offices

Private leagues are free and need no sign-up, so anyone can start one for a group chat, a classroom or an office. If you are running one for a larger group and something does not work the way you need, get in touch and say how many people are playing.

Advertising and partnerships

For advertising, sponsorship or partnership enquiries, use hello@triviverse.com with "Partnership" in the subject line.

Response times

Most messages get a reply within a few days. Reports of wrong answers in a live daily puzzle are looked at first, because everyone plays the same puzzle on the same day.
