# ads.txt — Authorized Digital Sellers (IAB Tech Lab spec)
#
# Declares who is allowed to sell advertising inventory on triviverse.com. Without
# it AdSense reports "ads.txt not found" and many buyers will not bid on the
# inventory at all, because unauthorised inventory is how domain spoofing works.
# It is not required for approval, but it is money left on the table once ads run.
#
# Fields: <exchange domain>, <publisher id>, <DIRECT|RESELLER>, <certification id>
# f08c47fec0942fa0 is Google's TAG certification authority id — the same value for
# every Google publisher, not a secret.
google.com, pub-2277295336282027, DIRECT, f08c47fec0942fa0
