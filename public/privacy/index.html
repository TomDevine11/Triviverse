Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

Triviverse ("we", "us") operates the football-trivia website at triviverse.com and related short-form video content. This policy explains what limited data we handle when you use the site.

1. Information we collect

Triviverse does not require an account, and we do not ask you for your name, email, or any other personal identifier to play. We collect only:

  • Analytics data — anonymised usage information (pages viewed, games played, approximate region, device/browser type) via Google Analytics 4, so we can understand which games people enjoy and improve them.
  • Local game state — your progress, streaks and settings are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. This never leaves your device and we cannot read it.

2. Cookies and similar technologies

We use a small number of cookies/identifiers, primarily for Google Analytics. These help us measure aggregate traffic. You can block or clear cookies in your browser at any time without losing the ability to play.

3. Third-party services

We use Google Analytics (Google LLC) to process anonymised usage statistics. Google acts as a processor and its use of data is governed by Google's own privacy policy. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

Some content is distributed via third-party platforms (for example TikTok and YouTube). When you view or interact with our content on those platforms, their own privacy policies apply to that activity, not this one.

4. How we use data

Only to operate and improve Triviverse — measuring which games and content are popular, diagnosing problems, and guiding what we build next. We do not use it to identify individual users.

5. Data retention

Aggregated analytics are retained for a limited period in line with Google Analytics' standard retention settings. Local game state remains on your device until you clear it.

6. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal accounts or directly identifiable information, there is normally nothing personal for us to retrieve or delete. You can clear your local data via your browser, and opt out of Google Analytics using Google's browser opt-out tools. If you have a question about your data, contact us (below).

7. Children

Triviverse is a general-audience trivia site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the current version.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@triviverse.com.
