About Triviverse

Triviverse is a collection of {{GAME_COUNT_WORD}} free daily football trivia games. Every game runs in your browser with no account, no download and no paywall, and a new matchday of puzzles starts for everyone at midnight UK time.

The games

  • Football Wordle: find a footballer’s surname in six guesses from coloured letter clues.
  • Football Tenable: name a top ten, such as the Premier League’s all-time scorers, before three mistakes.
  • Football Pointless: give correct answers that nobody else would think of.
  • Football Connections: sort sixteen players into four hidden groups.
  • Football Tic-Tac-Toe: name a player who fits both the row and the column, solo or against a friend.
  • Football Bingo: place each player dealt to you on a square he qualifies for, against the clock.
  • Football Higher or Lower: more or fewer goals, caps, appearances or transfer fees?
  • Football 501: count down from 501 by naming players, darts style.
  • Career Path: name the player from his clubs, revealed one at a time.
  • Guess the Footballer: name the player from the teammates he played alongside.
  • Football Contexto: find a hidden player using only how close each guess is.

Why it exists

Most football quizzes ask you to recall a fact. The games here try to be things you actually want to come back to the next day, with a shared daily puzzle, a streak worth protecting, and questions that reward knowing football rather than knowing trivia.

It started as a single game and grew into {{GAME_COUNT_WORD}}. Each game is a different angle on the same knowledge. Wordle tests whether you can spell a name, Career Path whether you remember a transfer, Pointless whether you remember the squad players nobody else does, and Tic-Tac-Toe whether you know where two parts of football history overlap.

How the questions are made

The questions are generated from one database of football records covering squads, transfers, league appearances and goals, international caps and honours, mostly drawn from Transfermarkt’s data. They are not written by hand one at a time. That is what makes a genuinely new puzzle possible every day across {{GAME_COUNT_WORD}} games.

The same records check your answers. If a player genuinely played for a club, including a short loan spell, he counts for it. If he never did, he does not, however much it feels like he should have. Each game also applies a recognisability threshold based on how much a player played and where, so the difficulty comes from the question rather than from obscure names.

Every daily puzzle is checked to be solvable before it goes out. Every Tic-Tac-Toe square has at least two well-known answers, every Connections board has exactly one solution, and every Bingo card can be completed inside the time.

Corrections

Generating questions from data means mistakes are possible. A record can be incomplete, a transfer can be dated wrongly, or a name can be spelled differently from how fans know it. If a question or answer looks wrong, please get in touch and say which game it was, the date you played it, and what you expected. Fixes are made in the underlying data, so a correction applies to every game that uses that record.

Who makes it

Triviverse is an independent project, not a company or a publisher network. It is built and run by one person in the United Kingdom, with no outside investment. Game design, data work and writing are all done in-house.

Keeping it free

The games are free and are intended to stay that way. Running costs are covered by advertising. How that works, and how your consent is handled, is set out in the Privacy Policy.
