About Triviverse

Triviverse is a collection of free daily football trivia games — Tenable, Pointless, Wordle, Connections, Tic-Tac-Toe, Higher or Lower, Career Path, Teammates and 501. Every game runs in your browser with no account, no download and no paywall.

Why it exists

Most football quizzes ask you to recall a fact. The games here try to be things you actually want to come back to the next day — a shared daily puzzle, a streak worth protecting, and questions that reward knowing football rather than knowing trivia.

It started as a single game and grew into nine. The name was chosen deliberately: Triviverse is not meant to stay a football-only site.

How the questions are made

The questions are generated from real football data — appearances, goals, transfers, caps and honours — rather than hand-written one at a time. That is what makes a genuinely new puzzle possible every day across nine games, and it means answers can be checked against the underlying record rather than someone's memory.

It also means mistakes are possible. If a question looks wrong, telling us is genuinely useful — see Contact.

Who makes it

Triviverse is an independent project, not a company or a publisher network. It is built and run by one person in the United Kingdom, with no outside investment.

Keeping it free

The games are free and are intended to stay that way. Running costs are covered by advertising — how that works, and how your consent is handled, is set out in the Privacy Policy.
